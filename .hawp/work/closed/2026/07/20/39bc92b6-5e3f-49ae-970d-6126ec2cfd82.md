## Outcome (filled at close)

Closed 2026-07-20. `hawp uuid [--short]`, `hawp links check`,
`hawp kit validate [--kit-path]`, `hawp work validate [--work-root|
--hawp-root]`, and the `backlog validate` alias are implemented in
`librarian/go/` across the Zacatl layers (domain/kit, domain/work,
application/{kit,work,links,uuidgen}, infrastructure/{repo,markdown}),
routed in platform/cli with an ExitError contract in cmd/hawp.

Notable divergence (bug fix, documented): the TS
`check-markdown-links.mjs` link regex used a lookbehind that matched
nothing, so it validated zero links. The Go `links check` actually checks
links; it blanks fenced code blocks and skips `.hawp/work/{closed,
evidence,notes,status}` archives to match the repo's dead-links policy.
Verified clean: 119 files.

  fence/archive policy fix; without it, it surfaced 52 stale archive links
  the TS checker silently missed — confirming the TS regex bug.
